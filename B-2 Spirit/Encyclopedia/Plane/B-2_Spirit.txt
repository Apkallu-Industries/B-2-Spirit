The Northrop Grumman B-2 Spirit is a multi-role stealth heavy bomber capable of delivering both conventional and nuclear munitions. A dramatic leap in technology, the B-2 represents a major milestone in the U.S. Air Force bomber modernization program. Its low-observable, or stealth, characteristics give it the unique ability to penetrate an enemy's most sophisticated defenses and threaten its most valued, and heavily defended, targets.

The B-2's blended wing/body configuration gives it a low radar cross-section. The bomber can fly at high subsonic speeds at altitudes up to 50,000 feet. Its four General Electric F118-GE-100 non-afterburning turbofan engines, each producing approximately 17,300 pounds of thrust, are buried within the wing to minimize infrared and radar signatures.

The B-2 has a crew of two pilots: the aircraft commander in the left seat and the mission commander in the right seat. The aircraft features fly-by-wire flight controls and a glass cockpit with four color multifunction displays.
